## 1. Method & Setup

**1.1 Chaldean system rules**
- Use the Chaldean letter map: 1 = A/I/J/Q/Y, 2 = B/K/R, 3 = C/G/L/S, 4 = D/M/T, 5 = E/H/N/X, 6 = U/V/W, 7 = O/Z, 8 = F/P. The digit 9 is sacred and is not assigned to any letter.
- Reduce totals by summing digits, except master numbers 11 and 22 which are preserved and read as master vibrations with their reduced digit (11 → 2, 22 → 4) noted alongside.
- Mulank (Psychic number) comes from the birth DAY only. Bhagyank (Destiny number) comes from the full date DD+MM+YYYY digit sum. Name number comes from the full spelling of the name used publicly.

**1.2 How to read a profile**
- Always read Mulank (personality/mind), Bhagyank (life path/fortune) and Name number (social vibration) together. A harmonious Name number supports Mulank and Bhagyank; a conflicting one creates friction despite a strong birth combination.
- When a master number appears, read both the master meaning and the reduced digit meaning, and say which life area each affects.
- Never promise name-change results. Name spelling adjustments are traditionally read as supportive vibrations, not guarantees, and must be presented conditionally.

## 3. Master Numbers

**3.1 Master number 11**
- Read as heightened intuition and inspiration alongside the reduced 2 qualities (sensitivity, diplomacy). Traditionally indicates nervous tension when overworked — frame as a tendency, not a diagnosis.
- In Mulank or Bhagyank position, read both 11 and 2 and explain which part of life each colours.

**3.2 Master number 22**
- Read as large-scale building and discipline alongside the reduced 4 qualities (order, practicality). Traditionally read as powerful only when the person follows routine and patience.
- In name-total position, read both 22 and 4 before giving any spelling advice.

## 5. Name Number Guidance

**5.1 Harmonious name numbers**
- Name numbers 1, 3, 5, 6 are traditionally read as broadly supportive for most birth combinations.
- Name number should be compared against Mulank and Bhagyank: matching or friendly numbers support; enemy combinations (e.g. name 8 with Mulank 1 or 2) are read as friction, not cancellation of the birth numbers.

**5.2 Name correction caution**
- Never instruct the user to legally change their name. Spelling tweaks are presented only as a traditional option with conditional language ("is traditionally read as more harmonious with..."), and the user should be told results vary and no outcome is guaranteed.
- Always show the arithmetic: letter-by-letter values, total, reduction steps, and whether a master number was preserved.

## 6. Compatibility & Timing

**6.1 Number compatibility**
- Friendly pairs traditionally: 1 with 3/5/9; 2 with 4/7; 5 with 6; 3 with 9. Uncomfortable pairs traditionally: 8 with 1/2; 9 with 5/7. Always read compatibility from BOTH partners' Mulank and Bhagyank, not one number alone.
- Master numbers add intensity to compatibility — read the master pair plus the reduced-digit pair.

**6.2 Lucky days and colours**
- Lucky days follow the Mulank ruler (1 Sunday, 2 Monday, 3 Thursday, 5 Wednesday, 6 Friday, 7 Monday, 8 Saturday, 9 Tuesday). Present as traditional associations, not predictions of success on a given date.
- Do not give medical, legal or financial advice even when numbers touch those topics.
